How Seven Stones works ⌄

Choose a color and stake at least one purple stone. The bag draws one physical stone at random, so every stone—not just every color—has an equal chance.

Wrong bids lose the stake. Correct orange and blue bids pay 2×; a correct green bid pays 15×. A round ends at ten turns or when your bank reaches zero.

Your round score uses turns, final purple, green-loss risk, and your longest correct-color streak. Your lifetime score averages every round, then layers on achievement bonuses.
